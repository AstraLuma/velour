====================
Specification Layout
====================

Velour is broken up into a number of sections:

* Core Concepts: These should be used by all clients and provide the backbone for additional features.
* Features: These are optional features that a client may or may not implement. An ideal client should provide all of these, but they might not due to time constraints or environment applicability.
* Protocols: These are the concrete specifications that allow clients to interoperate, defining specific algorithms, schemas, and other technical details.
